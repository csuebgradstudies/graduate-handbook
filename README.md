# Graduate Student Handbook

The Graduate Student Handbook of the CSU East Bay Office of Graduate Studies: university requirements, policies, and procedures for all graduate and post-baccalaureate students, including continuous enrollment, the capstone, and the University Thesis format review process.

The handbook is published at <https://csuebgradstudies.github.io/graduate-handbook/csueb-graduate-handbook.html>.

## Files

- [csueb-graduate-handbook.html](csueb-graduate-handbook.html): the handbook.
- [meta/handbooks.css](meta/handbooks.css): the stylesheet, on CSUEB visual identity, Montserrat and Roboto Serif on the brand palette.
